The Company & Archive

The Kingpin Entertainment Pty Ltd is the independent entertainment and creative platform behind DiggerMan, supporting music releases, visual presentation, media materials and the preservation of Riverlea-rooted creative history.

This website is intentionally built as a professional presenter and archive: large visual areas, clear editorial sections and generous room for photography, cover art, video stills and future documentation.
